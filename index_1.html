ABOUT THE COMPANY

A wholesale trading partner for PVC processors.

Oceair International Private Limited is a Wholesale Trader and Distributor of PVC Resin, PVC Regrind and related polymer materials, based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. The company sources both prime and off-grade PVC resin, along with regrind, pulverized powder and pipe scrap, for buyers manufacturing pipes, fittings and PVC profiles.

  • Sources from established international and domestic PVC producers, including material graded for pipe and general-purpose use.
  • Stocks PVC Regrind and Pulverized Powder in grey, white and mixed-colour grades for cost-sensitive production runs.
  • Also supplies Polyethylene Wax and Titanium Dioxide (Rutile) used as processing aids and pigments in plastics manufacturing.
  • Led by Director Kishan R. Thakkar, with day-to-day enquiries handled directly by phone and WhatsApp.
